Questions

What store owners ask us.

How much does it cost?+

Most WooCommerce migrations start at $7,500. Your exact price depends on your catalog size and what's connected to your store, which is why we ask two quick questions first. Your plan gives you one fixed price for the whole project, paid in three milestones. The price in your plan is the price you pay, unless you add to the scope.

Will Shopify cost more each month?+

It depends on your setup, so here's how it adds up. Shopify plans run $39 to $399 a month ($29 to $299 billed yearly) and include hosting, security, SSL and checkout, things you likely pay for separately today.7 Card fees on Shopify Payments start at the same 2.9% + 30¢ most WooCommerce stores pay through Stripe, and drop on higher plans. Apps are the part that varies, so we use Shopify's built-in features first, only add the apps your store really needs, and check with you before adding any paid app.

Am I locked in to Shopify?+

No. You own your products, customers, orders and content, and Shopify lets you export them anytime. You're not tied to us after launch either: the store, theme and accounts are yours.

What happens to my plugins?+

We review every plugin during the build and cover each one the leanest way that works: a built-in Shopify feature first, then an app, or custom work when it's needed. Sometimes that means testing a couple of options to find the right fit. You approve any paid app before it's added, and most stores end up with far fewer moving parts.

Can't I just use Shopify's import app?+

You can, and it's a good way to move products. It doesn't redirect your old URLs, rebuild your design, replace your plugins, protect your rankings or set up your product data for AI shopping. That's the difference between copying a store and launching one that works.

Have you worked with stores like mine?+

Our team has moved stores selling [categories, confirm with dev team]. What really shapes a migration is how your store works: product options, subscriptions, bundles, wholesale pricing and the systems connected to it. If anything about your products needs special handling on Shopify, including Shopify's own product restrictions, your plan flags it before you commit to anything.

Will my store go down?+

No. Your new store is built and tested on a staging site while WooCommerce keeps taking orders. We switch over once you've approved it.

Will I lose my Google rankings?+

A short adjustment period is normal after any platform move, so we won't promise nothing shifts. We promise protection: every URL mapped and redirected, content and metadata preserved, and 30 days of monitoring with fixes included.

Do customer accounts move?+

Accounts and order history move. Passwords usually can't transfer between platforms, so we set up a reactivation email that lets customers reset theirs in a click.

How long does a migration take?+

Most WooCommerce migrations take 4 to 8 weeks from kickoff to launch. Your plan gives dated milestones based on your store, and a project planned now can be built in January and launched before spring.

Will I have to learn a whole new system?+

Less than you'd think. Day to day, Shopify is simpler to run, with no plugins to update and no hosting to manage. Every project includes short video lessons on everyday tasks like adding products, processing orders and editing your homepage, plus a personal walkthrough recorded on your new store at launch.

Do I have to get on a call?+

Never required. The whole process works over email. If you'd like to talk, just say so and we'll set one up.
